Trails

Body

Biology and physics on the generated motion. Joint limits keep knees and elbows as hinges within human range; foot planting holds a foot still while it bears weight; floor keeps the body above the ground; balance keeps the centre of mass over the feet; smooth handovers blend the joins between generated pieces.

Phrases

The dancer remembers motifs (A, B, …) and develops them: repeats, mirrors, reverses, fragments, accumulates, varies. Lower variety ("repetitive") develops more; higher ("unpredictable") invents more.

          Text to motion

          With NVIDIA's Kimodo running on this machine, every step is generated from words (standing, walking a floor pattern, jumping, dancing) instead of borrowed from learned clips; MotionBricks moves the body through it.           Brain

          A language model reads freer language and imagery in prompts. Without one, a built-in movement vocabulary reads them. WebGPU models run in this browser (a one-time download, nothing leaves your machine); local servers need to allow this page's origin (for Ollama: OLLAMA_ORIGINS); cloud models use your own key, sent only to that provider.
