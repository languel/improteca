improteca

Inbetweener: generate motion through poses with NVIDIA MotionBricks, in the browser.

Loads the model (380 MB once, then cached) and dance clips from the mediathèque, makes random targets and plays in a loop.

Model

Not loaded. MotionBricks SOMA, FP16, 380 MB (cached after the first load).

Clips

BVH on the SOMA skeleton (used as is) or the CMU skeleton (retargeted onto SOMA). NVIDIA's samples are for evaluation only and load straight from their repository.

    Mediathèque

    Motion capture from languel/mediatheque. Tap a clip to add it.

      Poses

      Start, then targets in order. Each is a moment of a clip placed on the floor.
