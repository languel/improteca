improteca

An improvising dancer. Tell it how to dance: it chooses from movement it has learned and moves with NVIDIA MotionBricks, in your browser.

First run loads the motion model (380 MB, then cached) and learns dance from the mediathèque.

Phrases

The dancer remembers motifs (A, B, …) and develops them: repeats, mirrors, reverses, fragments, accumulates, varies. Lower variety ("repetitive") develops more; higher ("unpredictable") invents more.

      Motion capture

        Films

        The dancer watches a film (pose estimation with MediaPipe), fits its body to it and learns the movement. One camera: depth is approximate, travel is sideways only, jumps stay on the floor.

          Motion model

          Compare NVIDIA's FP16 model with improteca's 8-bit build (smaller and faster on CPU; it chooses slightly different but equally valid motion). Auto uses FP16 on WebGPU, 8-bit on WebAssembly.
